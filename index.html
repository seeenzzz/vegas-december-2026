Curated combinations / 03

Three ways to do Vegas.

01 · Dress up

The glamorous evening

  1. 4 PM — ARTE Museum
  2. 6 PM — CATCH dinner & Bellagio fountains
  3. 8 PM — Easy’s Cocktail Lounge
  4. 10 PM — The Vault (if open; smaller groups)

Best for photos and elegant outfits. Reserve the lounges in advance.

02 · First-time classics

Views & secrets

  1. Sunset — Eiffel Tower Viewing Deck
  2. 6 PM — High Roller
  3. 8 PM — Mott 32 or Din Tai Fung
  4. 10 PM — The Lock Speakeasy

Best for iconic skyline views and the hidden-door experience.

03 · Friendly rivalry

Play all day

  1. 12 PM — Activate Games
  2. 2 PM — Palette Tea Lounge or Istanbul Mediterranean 2
  3. 4 PM — Rest & change at Vdara
  4. 7 PM — Play Playground
  5. 10 PM — Ski Lodge or Ghost Donkey

Best for bonding, games and a relaxed finish. Split into teams.
